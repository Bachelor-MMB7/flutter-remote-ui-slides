# Limitations

**The measured span is not the full path.** From the tap arriving in the app to
the flushed frame — not from finger to glass.

**One setup.** One iPhone, one iOS version. Nothing here shows Android.

**Invented definitions.** Both variants were written for the demonstration, the
data is artificial, no warehouse operator worked with the system.

**Offline covers screens, not data.** Entered values are not persisted and do not
travel to a warehouse backend.

**App releases do not disappear.** Target API level requirements still force new
builds.
